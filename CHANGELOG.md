## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-15
### Added
- **Zero-Knowledge Folder Encryption (`.zenc` AES-256-GCM & WebAuthn Biometrics)** (`src/crypto/`, `FolderEncryptModal`, `FolderUnlockCard`, `FileSidebar`, `SettingsModal`, `server/`):
  - Client-side PBKDF2 (`310,000` iterations, SHA-256, NFC-normalized) and AES-256-GCM encryption into `[ZENC0001][12B IV][Ciphertext+Tag]` containers across all file types (`.md`, `.canvas`, `.base`, `.pdf`, images, and binary attachments); per-folder `.zuber-lock.json` metadata is managed over dedicated `/api/vault/folder-lock` endpoints while keys and plaintext remain strictly client-side.
  - Configurable auto-lock timeout (`5`, `15`, `30`, `60` minutes, or `Never` in Settings → Security), immediate lock on `beforeunload` or clicking `🔓` with IndexedDB cache purge (`purgeFolderNotes`), optional WebAuthn fingerprint/biometric unlock (`zuber_biometric_keys`), transparent in-editor editing of unlocked `.zenc` files, auto-encrypted folder attachment uploads (`<folder>/attachments/*.zenc`), and `blob:` URL decryption/cleanup in Live-Preview, Reading Mode, and Research Split-View.
